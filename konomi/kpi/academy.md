# KPI Metrics

Performance metrics for manufacturing — OEE, energy, production, reliability.

## Key Classes

- **OEE** — Overall Equipment Effectiveness (Availability × Performance × Quality)
- **Energy** — Energy consumption, efficiency, cost per unit
- **Production** — Throughput, yield, cycle time, batch count
- **Reliability** — MTBF, MTTR, failure rate, availability

## Core Concepts

- **OEE**: The gold standard. World-class = 85%+. Multiply Availability (uptime) × Performance (speed) × Quality (good parts)
- **Energy KPI**: kWh per unit produced, peak demand, power factor
- **MTBF/MTTR**: Mean Time Between Failures / Mean Time To Repair — the reliability pair
